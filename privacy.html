Privacy & data protection

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Who we are

What personal data we collect

We collect personal data that you give us directly, mainly through our contact and quote request form, by email, or by phone. This can include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Details of the property and the job you want quoted or carried out, including your address where you provide it
  • Any other information you choose to include in your message

If you become a customer, we also keep records connected to the work we carry out and invoice for, such as job details, dates, and payment records, for our accounting and legal obligations.

We do not use cookies, analytics, or other tracking technology on this website, and we do not collect payment card details through it.

How the contact form works

By default, our contact form opens your own email application with your message pre-filled, and you choose whether to send it. In that case, your message is sent directly from your email account to ours, the same as any other email, and does not pass through or get stored by any other website or company.

Why we use your data, and our legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide a quote — because it's necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract with you, or because we have a legitimate interest in responding to enquiries sent to us.
  • To carry out work you've booked, and invoice for it — because it's necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To keep financial and accounting records — because we have a legal obligation to do so (for example, under UK tax law).
  • To meet gas safety record-keeping requirements for gas safety checks and boiler work — because we have a legal obligation to do so.

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not sell your data.

Who we share your data with

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Subcontractors or engineers who need job details to carry out work you've booked
  • Our accountant or bookkeeper, for financial record-keeping
  • Regulators or authorities, where we're required to by law — for example, gas safety records
  • Service providers who support our email, hosting, or (if you add one) a form-handling service, acting on our instructions

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts' servers. Loading these fonts means your device's IP address is sent to Google in order to serve the font files. See Google's privacy policy for details of how Google handles this.

How long we keep your data

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you, and no longer than 12 months if no booking follows. For customers, we keep job and invoice records for at least 6 years to meet UK tax and accounting requirements, and gas safety records for at least 2 years, as required by gas safety regulations.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Ask us to delete your data, where we're not required to keep it for a legal reason
  • Ask us to restrict or object to certain processing
  • Ask for your data in a portable format, where relevant

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details above. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Keeping your data secure

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal data we hold, including limiting who can access it and using reputable email and hosting providers. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we aim to use practices appropriate to the modest amount of data we hold.

Children's data

Our services are aimed at adults arranging property work. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
