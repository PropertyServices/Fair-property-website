Common questions

How quickly will you reply?

We answer every enquiry within one working day, and send your fixed quote within 24 hours of having the details we need.

Do you charge for quotes or visits?

No. Quotes and site visits are free, and we don’t add call-out fees to invoices.

Are you insured?

Yes. All crew members are vetted and covered by our public liability insurance. We’re happy to send you a copy of the certificate.

Which areas do you cover?

We work within 30 km of our office. If you’re not sure whether you’re in range, send us your postcode and we’ll tell you.

Who pays for materials?

Materials and parts, such as paint or boiler components, appear on their own invoice line at the price we paid, with receipts available on request.

Can I book a regular service?

Yes. Regular boiler servicing and annual gas safety checks are priced lower than one-off visits. Mention it in your message and we’ll quote both.
