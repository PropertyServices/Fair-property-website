Spot the signs of pests

Small clues can point to a bigger issue. If any of these look familiar, we can help identify what's going on:

  • Droppings & Smells

    Droppings in cupboards, beneath sinks, or unusual smells along skirting boards.

  • Scratching Noises

    Scratching or movement in walls, lofts, under floorboards or ceilings at night.

  • Damage & Gnawing

    Gnaw marks on food packaging, woodwork, furniture, or electrical cables.

  • Bites & Marks

    Bites on skin or small blood spots on bedding and mattresses.
